Privacy Policy — VideoAgent

Last updated: 17 July 2026

Who we are

VideoAgent is a personal, locally-run tool used by a single individual (Juha Sundell, contact: juha.sundell@gmail.com) to produce and publish his own original short videos to his own social media accounts.

VideoAgent is not a service offered to other people. There are no other users, no accounts to create, and no data collected from anyone but the app owner himself.

Where the app runs

VideoAgent runs entirely on the owner's own personal computer. There is no server, no cloud backend, and no third-party data processor. Nothing you do on TikTok or YouTube is sent anywhere by this app.

What data the app accesses

The app accesses only the app owner's own data, and only for these purposes:

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not collect, store or process data about any other person.
  • It does not download, re-host or re-publish anyone else's videos, audio, music, images or text.
  • It does not scrape any platform, bypass any login, or evade any limit.
  • It does not sell, share, or transfer any data to anyone.
  • It does not publish anything automatically. Every upload requires the owner's explicit approval of that specific file.
  • It does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or tracking of any kind.

Third-party services

The app communicates only with the official APIs of TikTok and Google/YouTube, using official OAuth. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • TikTok: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

AI disclosure

Video scripts are drafted with a language model running locally on the owner's own machine, constrained to facts that have been verified against cited primary sources. Narration is synthetic text-to-speech of that original script — it is not a real person's voice and does not imitate any real person. This is disclosed in the videos' descriptions.

Your rights / control

Because the app processes only the owner's own data, there is no third-party data to request, correct or delete. The owner can at any time, from within the app:

  • see which accounts are connected and what permissions were granted
  • revoke access to any connected account
  • switch to a different account
  • immediately disable all publishing

Revoking access in the app deletes the stored tokens from the local DPAPI store. Access can also be revoked directly in TikTok's and Google's own account settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from them.
