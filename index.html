VideoAgent

A personal, locally-run tool for producing and publishing original short videos.

VideoAgent runs entirely on its owner's own computer. It has no server, no cloud backend, and no other users. It produces original short videos from fact-checked, cited sources and uploads them — only after the owner has explicitly approved that specific file — to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts via the platforms' official APIs.

  • No scraping, no login bypassing, no automated browsing of any platform.
  • No third-party video, audio, music or images are re-published.
  • Nothing is ever published automatically without the owner's per-file approval.
  • AI use (script drafting and synthetic narration) is disclosed in every video.
